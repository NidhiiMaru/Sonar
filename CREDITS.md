# Image & Video Credits — Sonar

Every visual asset shipped in this project is cleared for commercial use, including in a
judged competition. We deliberately shipped **only** assets that carry **no attribution
obligation** (US Government public domain, CC0, or Pexels/Coverr free licence) — the two
CC-BY bleaching images available to us were intentionally left out to keep this project free
of any licence conditions. Credit below is given as good practice, not obligation.
